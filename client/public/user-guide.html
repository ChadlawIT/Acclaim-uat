1. Signing In to the Portal

Accessing your Acclaim portal securely for the first time and on every visit.

The Acclaim portal is available at acclaim-api-prod-uks-001.azurewebsites.net/auth. Your account is created for you by Acclaim — you do not self-register.

The Acclaim portal login page — sign in with Microsoft or use your password.

Signing in with Microsoft (Recommended)

If your organisation uses Microsoft 365, you can sign in using your existing work account. This is the easiest and most secure method.

  1. 1
    Click "Sign in with Microsoft" You will be taken to Microsoft's secure sign-in page.
  2. 2
    Enter your work email address Use the same email address your Acclaim account was set up with.
  3. 3
    Complete Microsoft sign-in Enter your password (and MFA if required) as you normally would for any Microsoft 365 service.
  4. 4
    You're in You will be redirected straight to your dashboard.

Signing in with a Password

If you don't use Microsoft 365, or prefer to use a password, open the "Need help signing in?" section at the bottom of the login card and click "Sign in another way". This reveals the email and password fields.

On your very first login you will be given a temporary password by Acclaim. You will be prompted to set a new permanent password before you can access the portal.

💡 Forgot your password?

Click "Forgot password?" on the sign-in form. You'll receive a one-time code by email which lets you log in and set a new password. The code expires in 15 minutes.

5. Muting Cases

Control exactly which cases send you notifications, without losing access to any of them.

If your organisation has a large number of cases, you may find that you receive notification emails for every update — even cases that belong to a colleague and don't require your direct attention. Muting lets you silence those notifications on a case-by-case basis.

💡 Why muting is useful

Imagine you manage 50 cases but 35 of them belong to a colleague. Muting those 35 cases means your inbox only receives updates on your 15 — keeping your notifications focused and relevant.

How to Mute a Case

  1. 1
    Open the case Click the case from your Cases list to open its detail view.
  2. 2
    Find the bell icon at the top of the case A 🔔 bell icon appears in the case header, next to the case name.
  3. 3
    Click the bell to mute The icon changes to 🔕 (bell with a line through it) to confirm the case is muted. You will no longer receive email notifications for this case.
  4. 4
    To unmute — click it again Muting is never permanent. Click the 🔕 icon any time to restore notifications for that case.
Case header — mute toggle

Active · Pre-Legal Stage
🔕 Muted
⚠️ Notifications for this case are muted. You will not receive email updates for new messages or documents on this case.

Auto-Mute New Cases

You can also choose to automatically mute all new cases as they are added to your organisation. This means you start from silence and only unmute the cases you want to hear about. This setting is found in your Profile → Notification Preferences.

Unmuting to Cover for a Colleague

Muting is flexible. If a colleague is on leave and you need to take over their cases, simply open each of their cases and click the muted bell icon to unmute — you'll immediately start receiving updates for those cases. Once they return, you can mute them again.

📌 Important to know

Muting only affects your email notifications. The case is still fully visible to you and you can still read messages and documents — you just won't be emailed about new ones.

6. Sending Messages

Communicate securely with the Acclaim team — with or without attaching files.

The portal's messaging system replaces insecure email for communications with Acclaim. All messages are stored securely, linked to your cases, and both parties receive email alerts when a new message arrives.

Sending a Message from Within a Case

This is the most common way to send a message. Open a case, click the Messages tab, then click New Message. Your message will be automatically linked to that case, and the Acclaim team will see the full case context alongside your message.

Sending a General Message

For queries not related to a specific case, click Messages in the sidebar and then New Message. You can still choose to link it to a case, or leave it as a general enquiry.

Attaching Files to Messages

You can attach a file to any message. Supported formats include PDF, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx), and common image formats. The maximum file size is 10MB.

💡 Attachments are saved automatically

When you attach a file to a message linked to a case, it is automatically saved to the Documents section for that case. You don't need to upload it separately.

Replying to Messages

When Acclaim sends you a message, you will receive an email notification (unless the case is muted). Open the portal, navigate to the message, and click Reply. The original message text is quoted for context.

8. Submitting a New Case

Instruct Acclaim to begin recovering a new debt directly through the portal.

Click Submit a Case in the sidebar to open the case submission form. This sends a new instruction to Acclaim, complete with all the information needed to begin the recovery process.

What the Form Covers

SectionInformation Required
Client DetailsPre-filled from your profile — your name, email, and organisation.
Creditor InformationThe business or individual owed the money (usually you).
Debtor TypeIndividual / Sole Trader or Organisation (company). Different fields apply to each.
Debtor DetailsFull name or company name, address, contact details, company registration number (if applicable).
Debt DetailsOriginal amount owed, description of the debt, invoice date(s).
Payment TermsThe agreed payment terms — days from invoice, days from month end, or custom.
Invoice DetailsInvoice numbers, dates, and amounts.
Additional InformationAny relevant notes or background for Acclaim.
Supporting DocumentsUpload invoices, contracts, or correspondence to support your instruction.

💡 Only fill in what applies

The notification sent to Acclaim only includes the fields you have actually filled in — blank fields are excluded, keeping things clean and relevant.

Once submitted, Acclaim will review the instruction and create the case in the portal. You'll be able to track its progress from the Cases page.

10. Organisation Owner Controls

For users designated as the owner of their organisation — manage team access to cases.

If Acclaim has granted you Owner status for your organisation, you will see an additional item in the sidebar: Organisation Settings. This gives you tools to manage which members of your team can see which cases — without needing to contact Acclaim for every change.

👑 Am I an Owner?

If you are an Organisation Owner, your organisation name in the sidebar will show a small crown icon (👑) badge next to it. You will also see the Organisation Settings link in the sidebar.

Case Access Restrictions

The most powerful feature available to owners is the Case Access Matrix. This allows you to block or allow specific team members' access to individual cases.

What Restricted Users Cannot See

When a user is restricted from a case:

  • The case does not appear in their Cases list
  • They cannot access the case detail, even with a direct link
  • They do not receive message or document notifications for that case
  • The restriction is invisible to them — there is no error shown, the case simply doesn't exist for them

Restrictions can be removed at any time from the same matrix view.

Viewing Your Team's Portfolio

The Organisation Settings page also gives you a full view of all cases in your organisation and which team members are associated with each one, so you always have an accurate picture of your team's workload.

11. Reports

Export detailed analysis of your debt recovery portfolio in Excel and PDF.

Click Reports in the sidebar to access all available reports. Each report can be filtered, viewed on screen, and exported to Excel or PDF.

📋

Case Summary Report

A complete overview of your portfolio — debtor names, references, statuses, stages, original debt, costs, interest, and outstanding balances for every case.

📈

Recovery Analysis Report

Metrics on your recovery effectiveness over time — recovery rates, trends, and performance by stage.

💷

Payment Performance Report

A full breakdown of cash flow — payments received, expected, and overdue, with patterns over time.

📅

Monthly Statement Report

A month-by-month reconciliation of all activity — useful for accounting and financial reporting purposes.

Filtering Reports

Before generating a report, you can filter by:

  • Organisation — if you belong to multiple organisations, report on one or all
  • Case status — active cases only, closed cases only, or all
  • Date range — for time-period specific analysis

Exporting

Every report can be exported as an Excel spreadsheet (with colour-coded status columns) or as a PDF for sharing or printing. Exports include all filtered data and the same statistics shown on screen.

💡 Scheduled Reports

Acclaim can configure scheduled reports to be emailed to you automatically — daily, weekly, or monthly. If you'd like to set this up, contact Acclaim at email@acclaim.law and they can configure it from the admin panel on your behalf.
